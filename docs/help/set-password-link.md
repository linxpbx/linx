# Your invite or set-password link

Your admin gives you a link (or a QR code) to set up your Linx account. It works once, for 24 hours.

## Setting up

1. Open the link, or scan the QR code with your phone's camera.
2. **Welcome to Linx**: Choose how you'll sign in:
   - **Passkey** (recommended): Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello or your phone. Nothing to remember or type.
   - **Password + authenticator app**: a password, then a 6-digit code from an app on your phone.
   - **Password only**, if your admin allows it: simplest, but a leaked password is enough to get in.
3. Follow the steps. Save your recovery codes somewhere safe: they won't be shown again.

![Choosing how to sign in](screen:setup-password)

See [Passkeys and authenticator apps](passkeys-and-authenticator) for more on each.

## This link can't be used

The link expired or was already used. Ask your admin for a new one: they can make one from People.

## A link when you already have an account

If your admin sends a new link after you forgot your password, it sets a new password only. You're still asked for your passkey or authenticator code afterwards: a link never skips that step.

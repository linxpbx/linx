# Voicemail

When nobody answers, callers can leave a message. **Voicemail** in the sidebar shows yours, newest first, with a number on it for the ones you haven't heard yet.

![Voicemail](screen:voicemail)

## Listening

- **New** messages are at the top with a dot. Press ▶ to listen; a message you play to the end moves to **Heard**.
- **Call back** rings the caller from your browser.
- **⋯** has **Mark as new** (or **Mark as heard**), **Download** (a WAV file every computer and phone plays) and **Delete**.
- If you're in a [ring group](ring-groups), its messages show here too, marked "for Sales". When someone in the group has heard one, the others see "Heard by Sara". Deleting a group's message deletes it for everyone in the group.
- The box at the top (**Mine**) lets you look at one group's messages only. Admins also get **All boxes**; when an admin listens to someone else's message, it's written in the activity log (System → Activity).

Messages are kept for 60 days and then deleted (an admin can change that, below). Each message can be up to 3 minutes long.

## Your greeting

Choose **Settings** at the top of Voicemail.

![Voicemail settings](screen:voicemail-settings)

- **Greeting**: **Linx's own** says *"The person you called isn't available. Please leave a message after the tone."* **Record** your own instead: press **Record**, speak, press **Stop**, listen with **Play**, then **Use this**. A greeting can be up to 30 seconds. Linx uses the microphone chosen in [Settings](microphone-and-speaker).
- **Outside office hours**: what callers hear when a number's [office hours](office-hours) send them to your voicemail while you're closed. Linx's own says *"We're closed right now. Please leave a message after the tone."*
- You can go back to **Linx's own** at any time; your recording is kept, so you can switch back to it.

![Recording a greeting](screen:voicemail-record)

## By email

**Email me new messages** sends each new message to your email address with the recording attached, so you can listen on your phone. It's greyed until an admin sets up [email](email). A ring group's messages aren't emailed: everyone in the group sees them here.

## Turning voicemail off

The **Voicemail** switch in Settings turns your voicemail off: callers who aren't answered then hear "not available" and can't leave a message. Messages you already have stay.

## For admins

- **People**: each person's details show their voicemail ("On · 3 messages · email on") with **Turn off**.
- **Ring groups**: a group's details have its voicemail: on or off, and its own greetings. Anyone in the group can record them.
- **System → Settings**: **Keep voicemail** sets how many days messages are kept (7 to 365), and shows the space voicemail uses. Messages older than that are deleted within the hour.
- Where a call goes is set in [Incoming calls](incoming-numbers) and [Ring groups](ring-groups). When someone's voicemail is off, the sentence there says so: *"callers hear "not available" (the voicemail for Sara Haddad (101) is off)."*
- Voicemail is kept in Linx's database, so [backups](backups) include it.

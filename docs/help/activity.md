# Activity

System → **Activity** shows what happened in Linx, as sentences: who signed in, who changed what, and when. Open one to see its **Details**. Passwords and keys are never shown.

![Activity](screen:system-activity)

- Choose a period: **Last 24 hours**, **Last 7 days** or **Last 30 days**.
- Search, or choose who.
- **On the server** means a command run on the server itself.

Something you don't recognise, like a sign-in from an unknown place? Change that person's password and check their [signed-in browsers](my-account#signed-in-browsers).

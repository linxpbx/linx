# Office hours and holidays

Office hours say when your company is open. A phone number can ring people during them and do something else outside them, like play "We're closed" ([Incoming calls](incoming-numbers)).

![Office hours](screen:office-hours)

The times are the server's time zone, the one chosen at install. The page says which one, and **Now: open** or **Now: closed** at the top is the same answer a real call gets at this moment.

## The week

A business gets **Office hours** when it's set up: Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00. To change them, go to **Office hours**:

- Turn a day on or off with its switch.
- Choose when it opens and closes. **24:00 (midnight)** means open until the end of that day.
- **+** adds another time on the same day, for a lunch break: 08:00–13:00 and 14:00–17:00.
- **Copy Monday to every open day** gives every open day Monday's times.

Then choose **Save hours**.

## Holidays

On a holiday it's closed all day, whatever the week says. Choose **+ Add** under **Holidays**, give it a name and its first day (and its last day, for several days).

Tick **Every year on the same dates** for a fixed holiday like National Day. Eid moves every year, so add it again each year.

Holidays that are over move under **Past holidays**.

## At home

At home there are no office hours: every number rings the same all the time. **Add office hours** adds them if you want them anyway.

![No office hours at home](screen:office-hours-home)

## More schedules

**More schedules** (at the bottom) adds another set of hours, for example **Support hours**. A number then chooses which hours it follows. A schedule a number follows can't be removed until that number follows other hours.

## Checking it

To see what a call to one of your numbers does on a given day and time, use the [call simulator](calling-permissions#the-call-simulator) and choose **On** under **When**.

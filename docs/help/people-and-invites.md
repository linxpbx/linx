# People and invite links

People are who sign in to Linx: to make calls, or to help run it.

![People](screen:people)

## Adding someone

**+ Add**, then:

- **Quick add**: just a name and email. Change the rest later.
- **Guide me**: a few short steps, each explained.

Choose the type:

- **Person**: someone who signs in to make and take calls. Gets an invite link.
- **Admin**: a person who can also manage Linx (people, extensions, phone lines). Adding one asks you to confirm it's you.
- **Phone**: an extension for a phone that isn't someone's, like a door phone or a meeting room. No email.

Linx gives them the next free number in the people range.

## The invite link

After **Create**, Linx shows an invite link and a QR code. It works once, for 24 hours. You won't see it again, so send it now (**Copy link**), then **I've saved it**. Lost it? Open the person and choose **New invite link**.

What they do with it: [Your invite or set-password link](set-password-link).

## Changing someone

Open the person to change their **Name**, **Email**, **Role** or extension.

## Danger zone

- **Reset authenticator**: when they lost their phone and recovery codes ([Lost your authenticator or passkey](lost-authenticator)). It signs them out everywhere.
- **Unlock**: ends a wait after too many wrong passwords ([Locked out](locked-out)).
- **Disable**: they'll be signed out everywhere and their browser phone stops working.

An admin can't change a system admin: only a system admin can.

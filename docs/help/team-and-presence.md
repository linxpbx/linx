# Team and presence

**Team** lists everyone with a Linx account: their name, extension and status. Press **Call** to ring someone.

![Team](screen:team)

## Your status

Choose your status from the menu under your name:

- **Available**: calls ring as usual.
- **Away**: you're not at your desk.
- **Do not disturb**: others see you don't want calls.

Others also see when you're on a call.

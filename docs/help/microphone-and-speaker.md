# Microphone, speaker and ringtone

**Settings** (in the sidebar) chooses which **Microphone** and **Speaker** the browser uses for calls, and the **Ringtone volume**. These apply to this browser only.

![Settings](screen:settings)

- **Browser default** follows your computer's own choice.
- **Test sound** plays a sound on the chosen speaker.

To check both together, call `*43`, the echo test ([Calls in the browser](calls-in-the-browser#the-echo-test)).

A headset you plug in during a call may need choosing here. If the list is empty or has no names, allow the microphone in your browser first.

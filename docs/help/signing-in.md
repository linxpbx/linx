# Signing in

Open your Linx's address, like `https://pbx.example.com`, and sign in.

![The sign-in page](screen:signin)

## With a password

Type your **Email** and **Password**, then **Sign in**. If you have a second step, Linx asks for it next:

- your passkey (Face ID, your fingerprint or your device's PIN), or
- the **6-digit code from the app** in your authenticator app, or
- one of your recovery codes, with **Use a recovery code instead**.

## With a passkey

Choose **Sign in with a passkey**. Your device asks for Face ID, your fingerprint or its PIN, and you're in. Nothing to type. Passkeys only work at your Linx's own address.

## With your company account

If your admin set it up, a button like *Sign in with Google* appears. Your Linx email must be the same as your company account's email.

## Something went wrong

- **Wrong email or password.** Check both. After several wrong tries, Linx makes you wait before the next one, even with the right password. See [Locked out](locked-out).
- **Your sign-in timed out.** You took too long on the second step. Enter your password again.
- Lost your phone or your passkey? See [Lost your authenticator or passkey](lost-authenticator).
- More help: **Help signing in**, under the sign-in box, opens these guides without signing in.

# Signing in

Open your Linx's address, like `https://pbx.example.com`, and sign in.

![The sign-in page](screen:signin)

## With a password

Type your **Email** and **Password**, then **Sign in**. If you have a second step, Linx asks for it next:

- your passkey (Face ID, your fingerprint or your device's PIN), or
- the **6-digit code from the app** in your authenticator app, or
- one of your recovery codes, with **Use a recovery code instead**.

## With a passkey

Choose **Sign in with a passkey**. Your device asks for Face ID, your fingerprint or its PIN, and you're in. Nothing to type. Passkeys only work at your Linx's own address.

## With your company account

If your admin set it up, a button like *Sign in with Google* appears. Your Linx email must be the same as your company account's email.

## Forgot your password?

If your Linx can send email, **Forgot your password?** appears under the password box.

1. Choose **Forgot your password?**, type your email, then **Send the link**. The page always says the same thing, whether or not that email has an account here: nobody can use it to find out who has one.
2. Open the email *Choose a new Linx password*. Its link works once, for 30 minutes. Nothing arrived? Check spam, or ask your admin. At most 3 links an hour go to one email.
3. Type your new password twice, then **Continue**.
4. If you have a second step, Linx asks for it now: your authenticator code, your passkey or a recovery code. Your password only changes once that's done, so someone who got into your mailbox alone can't change it.
5. Done: you're signed in, and signed out everywhere else. Linx emails you *Your Linx password was changed*. If you didn't do it, tell your admin at once.

A used or older link says so; choose **Send a new one**. Lost your authenticator app, passkeys and recovery codes as well? Email can't replace those. Choose **Ask my admin to reset it** under the code: your admins are told, check it's really you, and reset your second step. Then use **Forgot your password?** again. See [Lost your authenticator or passkey](lost-authenticator). If your Linx requires company sign-in, no link is sent: use the company button.

No **Forgot your password?** on the page? Your Linx can't send email yet: ask your admin for a set-password link ([Your invite or set-password link](set-password-link)).

## Something went wrong

- **Wrong email or password.** Check both. After several wrong tries, Linx makes you wait before the next one, even with the right password. See [Locked out](locked-out).
- **Your sign-in timed out.** You took too long on the second step. Enter your password again.
- Lost your phone or your passkey? See [Lost your authenticator or passkey](lost-authenticator).
- More help: **Help signing in**, under the sign-in box, opens these guides without signing in.

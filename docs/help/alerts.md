# Alerts

Linx tells you when something needs you: a phone line down, a failed backup, someone guessing passwords, unusual calls abroad.

![Alerts](screen:system-alerts)

## Where alerts go

**+ Add** → **Guide me**, then choose where: **ntfy** (recommended for phones: a free app, alerts arrive as notifications), **Telegram**, **Slack**, **Microsoft Teams**, **Gotify** or a signed webhook for your own software.

## Which alerts

- **Only critical**: only when calls can't be made or Linx needs you now.
- **Problems** (recommended): also warnings.
- **Everything**: also notes, like a first call to a new country.

## Quiet hours

**Not at night** holds other alerts during the hours you choose. Critical ones are always sent.

## Test it

The last step sends a test. Check it arrived where you chose, then **It arrived**. **Send a test** again any time from its page.

## Alerts on the page

Admin home's **Needs attention** shows open alerts. Each clears itself when the problem is fixed.

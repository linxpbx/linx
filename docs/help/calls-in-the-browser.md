# Calls in the browser

Linx is a phone in your browser. Keep the page open to get calls.

![The Dialer](screen:dialer)

## Making a call

In the **Dialer**, type a name, extension or number, then press the call button. Or search at the top of any page (**Search people or dial a number**), or press **Call** next to someone in [Team](team-and-presence).

The first time, your browser asks to use the microphone. Allow it.

## Getting a call

The call appears with the caller's name or number. Press **Answer** or **Decline**. If you have Linx open in several tabs, only one of them rings.

## During a call

- **Mute** and **Unmute** your microphone.
- **Keypad** for menus that ask you to press a number.
- **End call** to hang up.

Audio is encrypted all the way between your browser and Linx.

## The echo test

Call `*43`. Linx plays back what you say, so you can check your microphone and speaker. If you hear nothing, see [No audio on a call](no-audio).

## Recent calls

**Recent** lists your last 5 calls, from all your phones and browsers. **All calls** opens [Call history](call-history).

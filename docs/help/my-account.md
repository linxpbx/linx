# My account

Open it from the menu under your name: **My account**.

![My account](screen:account)

## Your email

**Change**, type the **New email** and confirm it's you. You'll sign in with the new one from now on.

## Your password

**Change password**: type your **Current password**, then the **New password** and **Type it again**. Changing it signs you out everywhere, this browser too. Then you sign in again, everywhere, with the new one. Your passkeys and authenticator app don't change.

No password yet? **Add a password**: it lets you sign in from a device that doesn't have your passkey.

## Passkeys and the authenticator app

- **Add a passkey**, **Rename** or **Remove** one. You can't remove your only way to sign in.
- **Authenticator app**: set one up, or replace it (after a new phone or when your recovery codes run out). See [Passkeys and authenticator apps](passkeys-and-authenticator).

## Company account

Link your Google or Microsoft account to sign in with it instead of a password, if your admin set it up.

## Signed-in browsers

Every browser and phone where you're signed in. **Sign out** one you don't recognise or no longer use, or **Sign out everywhere else**.

Some changes ask you to **Confirm it's you** first: your password and second step, or your passkey.

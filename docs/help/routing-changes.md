# Undo and routing changes

Every change to where calls go is kept: numbers and lines, ring groups, office hours and holidays, **When someone calls**, and outgoing calls (the order lines are tried and what each calling level allows). A mistake is one click away from being put right.

## Saved. Undo

After each change, **Saved.** and **Undo** show at the bottom of the page for 10 seconds.

![Saved. Undo](screen:undo-toast)

- **Undo** puts it back as it was just before. Then **Redo** puts your change back again.
- If someone else changed the routing since, Undo stops and points you to **Routing changes**, so their change isn't undone too.

## Routing changes

System → **Routing changes** lists the last 50 changes: when, who, and what.

![Routing changes](screen:system-routing-changes)

- **See the change** shows it before and after, in the same sentences as the screens ("Calls ring Sara Haddad, then Reception, 15 seconds each").
- **Put this back** puts the routing back to how it was just before that change. It first shows what that will change now. Putting a version back is a new line in the list, so it can be undone too.

![Put this back](screen:system-routing-changes-put-back)

Good to know:

- Putting back an older version undoes every change after it too. Check the list it shows first.
- A ring group added since is removed, with its voicemail messages. A ring group removed since comes back, with an empty voicemail.
- Someone removed since stays removed: their calls go to "not available".
- Phone lines and numbers themselves aren't put back, only who they ring. A line added since keeps its place for outgoing calls, after the others.
- Turning calls abroad or premium-rate calls back on asks you to [confirm it's you](my-account), as when you change it directly.
- Reporters see the list but can't put anything back.
